## 11. Educational Notes — Recommendation Rationale

### Why This Stack? (For Learning Purposes)

**Next.js 15 (App Router)** was chosen because investor-facing sites need excellent SEO. Server-side rendering means search engines can crawl Katherine's credentials, and the App Router's server/client component model teaches modern React architecture.

**TypeScript** catches errors at compile time — essential when building a professional site where broken links or undefined data could cost an investor interaction.

**Tailwind CSS** enables rapid iteration on design without context-switching to CSS files. The utility-first approach maps directly to design tokens, making it easy to maintain a consistent visual language.

**Framer Motion** elevates the experience from "website" to "digital product." Subtle entrance animations, scroll-triggered reveals, and counter animations communicate attention to detail — exactly what investors look for in a founder.

**Vercel** provides zero-friction deployment with automatic HTTPS, global CDN, and preview deployments for every PR. The free tier is sufficient for a portfolio site.

### Why This Content Strategy?

Katherine's career is *inherently narrative* — she went from producing Emmy-winning TV to acquiring IP rights to building an AI company that manages IP. This is not a random pivot; it's a story of deepening expertise applied to a massive market opportunity.

The portfolio structures this narrative in three acts:
1. **Credibility** (Emmy wins, £20M+ projects, 50+ countries) → "She can execute"
2. **Domain Expertise** (Primeval IP acquisition, rights management) → "She knows this space intimately"
3. **Vision** (SILOETT.AI, Andrew Ng courses, Oxford AI Governance, Station F) → "She's building the future of it"

This narrative arc mirrors how the best investor pitches work: establish trust, demonstrate domain knowledge, then paint the vision.

### Why Station F Matters

Station F in Paris is the world's largest startup campus, founded by Xavier Niel in 2017. Housing 1,000+ startups with partners including Google, Microsoft, Meta, LVMH, and more, it is a globally recognized signal of startup credibility. Being a Station F founder communicates that SILOETT has been vetted and accepted into a competitive ecosystem that has produced notable alumni including Hugging Face.
